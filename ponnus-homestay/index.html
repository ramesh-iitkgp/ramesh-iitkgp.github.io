Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about staying at Ponnus Homestay near Sabine Hospital.

How close is Ponnus Homestay to Sabine Hospital?

Ponnus Homestay is located just 200 meters from Sabine Hospital & Research Centre. It takes less than 2 minutes of relaxed walking on a flat hospital access road, making it ideal for patients who should avoid bumpy vehicle travel.

Do you provide home-cooked food suitable for patients?

Yes! We take special pride in preparing hygienic, traditional home-cooked Kerala food. For patients undergoing fertility or medical treatments, we can tailor salt, spice, and oil levels as per dietary advice.

Is the homestay safe for solo women travelers and female attendants?

Absolutely. Ponnus Homestay is specifically curated with a focus on women's safety. We have 24/7 CCTV surveillance across all entryways and corridors, secure locking mechanisms, and a warm, supportive management presence.

Are there discounted packages for long-term stays (2 to 4 weeks)?

Yes. For patients and families staying 10 days, 2 weeks, or a full month for medical treatment, we offer special discounted long-stay rates. Contact us directly on WhatsApp to get a custom long-stay quote.

How do I book a room and what are the payment terms?

Booking is simple and instant via WhatsApp. Click any booking button on this site to select your dates and room. You can pay via UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe), net banking, or cash upon arrival with zero advance commission.
