Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about planning your stay in Chinnakanal, Munnar.

Where is Ponnus Homestay located in Munnar?

Ponnus Homestay is located in Chinnakanal village, near Balu Stores, around 18 km from Munnar town along the newly widened and scenic Gap Road. Chinnakanal is known for its higher elevation (5,800 ft), cooler climate, and proximity to Kolukkumalai and Anayirankal Dam.

Can you arrange the Kolukkumalai Sunrise 4x4 Jeep Safari?

Yes! We arrange private 4x4 off-road safari jeeps that pick you up directly from our homestay at 4:30 AM to reach Kolukkumalai Peak in time for the golden sunrise above the clouds. You can select the safari option while booking via WhatsApp.

Is campfire and barbecue available?

Yes, we arrange evening bonfires / campfires in our outdoor courtyard, complete with acoustic music and live charcoal barbecue on request. It is the perfect highlight for chilly mountain nights.

Do you have 24-hour hot water and Wi-Fi?

Yes. Chinnakanal gets pleasantly cold in the evenings and mornings, so every attached bathroom is fitted with an instant water geyser ensuring hot showers 24/7. High-speed Wi-Fi is also provided free of charge.

How do we confirm our booking?

Booking is direct and instant via WhatsApp. Click any booking button to select your dates and room. You receive direct owner pricing with zero online portal commissions, and payment can be made via UPI or cash upon arrival.
