Our Heritage

The Soul of the Tinnai

The concept of the Tinnai is deeply rooted in Tamil culture. In ancestral village homes, the Tinnai is the raised front veranda open to the street—where passing travelers rest their feet, neighbors gather to chat, and everyone is greeted with a hearty meal.

Tableside Hot Gravy Trolley

Our custom-built brass trolley rolls between tables, ladling boiling hot mutton, chicken, fish, and crab gravies onto your banana leaf as often as you desire.

Farm-Fresh Cold Pressed Oils

We strictly cook with traditional cold-pressed sesame (nallennai) and groundnut oils with no artificial additives or preservatives.

Guests Treated Like Family

No formal barriers or rush—relax on our wooden benches, enjoy the aroma of roasted spices, and eat with your hands the way it was meant to be.
