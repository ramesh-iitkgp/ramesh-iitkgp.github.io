A few little details.

How does this demo work?

Explore the catalogue, pick a pack size and add items to your basket. Checkout creates a sample order summary in your browser. It is not sent to a shop and no payment is collected.

Can I change my basket?

Yes. Change quantities, remove items or choose another pack size. Your basket is remembered on this device when browser storage is available.

What about delivery and payments?

This demo shows free delivery from ₹999, otherwise ₹60. These are sample terms. Live delivery coverage, online payments and order management can be configured for the real shop.

Do these products contain allergens?

The sample range includes tree nuts. Ingredients and cross-contact information must be confirmed with the shop before purchasing real products.
